containerd-registry

Helm chart repository for containerd-registry, a pull-through registry whose storage is the containerd of its node.

helm repo add containerd-registry https://ruakij.github.io/containerd-registry
helm repo update
helm install containerd-registry containerd-registry/containerd-registry -n kube-system

Charts are also published as OCI artifacts at oci://ghcr.io/ruakij/charts/containerd-registry, which is where prereleases go. The index here lists stable releases only.
